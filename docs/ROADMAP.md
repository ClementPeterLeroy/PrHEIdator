# Roadmap

These are proposed milestones, not promises or historical releases.

## v2.0 — Game Rebirth

Delivered locally: modular application, one responsive loop, the five
implemented school modes, corrected asset paths and game-over/navigation
bugs, lightweight tests, archived original source and project documentation.

Before the owner publishes: playtest with people, confirm asset provenance,
choose an appropriate source-code license and review the local changes.
Version control and publishing remain the owner's responsibility.

## v2.1 — Playtesting and usability

- Compare long matches with the archived source and review documented
  collision symmetry and corner-boundary corrections.
- Validate four simultaneous players on physical keyboards and audio on
  real devices, plus supported Python/OS combinations.
- Add remappable controls and saved volume/settings if the owner approves.
- Offer reduced flashing/intro accessibility settings while preserving the
  original presentation as an available choice.
- Resolve asset credits/permissions and reduce unused assets only after review.

## v2.2 — Distribution and maintenance

- Evaluate packaging for agreed desktop platforms and clean-install testing.
- Add CI checks after the owner establishes the public repository.
- Improve reactive AI tunability and regression coverage using playtest
  findings, without introducing an AI training system.
- Decide whether Fun should be designed as a separately scoped feature or
  retired from the menu; it remains unimplemented in v2.0.

No online multiplayer, new engine or neural-network AI is part of this work.
